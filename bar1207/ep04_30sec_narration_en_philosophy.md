# EP04 — 30 Second English Narration (Philosophy Cut)

**Character:** Clara Webb — 2017 wellness copywriter  
**Duration:** ~28–32 seconds · **Tone:** Quiet, literary, anti-performative — not inspirational  

---

## Narration Script (English · Philosophy Cut)

```
I used to think peace was something you subscribed to —
ten minutes a day, a voice telling you when to breathe.

But the mind doesn't invoice you for stillness.
It only asks: are you here, or are you rehearsing being here?

Rain found a door that wasn't on any map.
Inside, a clock at twelve-oh-seven — not late, not early, just between.

No guru. No script. An invisible menu I couldn't read until I stopped trying.

I didn't become better.
I became present — which, I think, is the finer thing.
```
