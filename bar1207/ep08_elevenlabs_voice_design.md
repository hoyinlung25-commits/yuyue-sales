# EP08 — Voice Prompts · Aster · Young Star

**Character:** Aster · young star · English male vocal only  
**Lock:** Clear · sincere · boyish · emotional age **13–16**  

---

## Master Voice Design（ElevenLabs + Mureka）

```text
Create a young English male voice with emotional coloring of age 13-16 — not a small
child, not an adult man. Clear, sincere, bright tenor with boyish honesty.

Character: Aster, a young star who chooses to fall so a girl remembers tomorrow
is still worth reaching. Mission, sacrifice, wonder — not comedy, not romance seduction.

Tone: Cinematic fantasy protagonist. Literary and true. Like a young hero in a
Studio Ghibli-adjacent English dub done seriously — sincere not cute, earnest not preachy.

Pace: Measured on verses, modest swell on chorus. Never screaming, never whisper-ASMR child.

Texture: Clean consonants, open vowels, light chest voice, no vocal fry, no baby lisp.

STRICT AVOID: nursery rhyme sing-song, toddler voice, adult baritone seduction, female
voice, duet partner, anime shonen scream, gospel belting, robotic TTS.
```

### Test Lines（建 voice 後）

```
I was taught to watch — not to fall.

If falling means I never go home…
I still choose to burn the small self I was.
Tomorrow is still worth reaching.
```
