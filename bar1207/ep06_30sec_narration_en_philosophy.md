# EP06 — 30 Second English Narration (Philosophy Cut)

**Character:** Damien · a devil who loves an angel  
**Duration:** ~28–32 s · **Tone:** Quiet confession · not villain monologue  

---

## Narration Script

```
I was made for shadow. She was made for dawn.
I never wanted her to fall — I only wanted to love her,
which heaven calls sin and hell calls useless.

I followed her light once, through rain that wasn't mine.
I found a door with no sign. A clock at twelve-oh-seven.
No choir. No trial. Just amber light thick enough to hold the truth.

I won't ask her to descend.
I won't pretend I'm good.
I would still choose her — from below her light, without a claim.

That is my love. That is my night.
```
