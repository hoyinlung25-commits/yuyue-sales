## Story

He was made for shadow. She was made for dawn.  
He did not want to corrupt her — he only wanted to **love** her,  
which heaven calls the oldest sin and hell calls useless.

One night he saw her light move across the city like a comet.  
He followed — not to touch, only to stay near enough to remember what grace felt like.

Rain. A door without a sign. **Twelve-O-Seven.**  
Clock at 12:07. Amber candles. No judgment.

He sat. He did not pray. He did not blaspheme.  
He only admitted, quietly, what no underworld or paradise allows:

*I love her. I would still choose her.*

**Visual:** Black coat · amber bar · angel wings in window reflection only  
**Vocal:** English male · gothic romantic · clear literary tone
