## Story Core · 故事核心

**Aster** is a young star — not ancient, not a god — still learning what light is for.

Rain leads him to a door with no sign. **Twelve-O-Seven.** Clock at 12:07.  
The bar teaches him: light's mission is not to stay in the sky forever,  
but to become a path when someone cannot see tomorrow.

From the rain window he sees **Mira**: gray coat, canvas bag, head down —  
not crying loudly, just **almost** letting go of tomorrow.

The sky has one law: **if you fall, you never go home.**  
He chooses to **burn the small self** anyway.

Mira is not magically saved. She simply takes **one more step**.

**Aster** = watcher · **Mira** = the one hope touches  
Not romance · not parent and child · **light and the person light finds**
