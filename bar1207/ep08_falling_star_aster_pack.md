# EP08《Burn the Small Light》— Aster · A Young Star's Sacrifice

**中文核心：** 年輕的星 · 為希望墜落 · 在 12:07 明白光的使命  
**Full title:** *Burn the Small Light · Tomorrow Is Still Worth Reaching*

**Touched:** **Mira** — the girl who almost lost hope  
**Signature:** 流星墜落便不能回家 — 他仍選擇燃盡小我，讓人記起明天仍值得抵達  
**Positioning:** English · fate · duty · sacrifice · **多走一步**  

---

## Story Core · 故事核心

**Aster** is a young star — not ancient, not a god — still learning what light is for.

One night he watches a city below and sees **Mira**: a girl standing in rain,  
not crying loudly, just **almost** letting go of tomorrow.

Aster has always been told: *watch, don't interfere.*  
The sky has one law for stars like him: **if you fall, you never go home.**

He asks the sky anyway. The answer is silence — then warning.

Rain leads Mira to a door with no sign. **Twelve-O-Seven.** Clock at 12:07.  
Aster's light reaches the window — not to save her with magic,  
but to remind her: **one more step is still allowed.**

He chooses to **burn the small self** — the star that wanted to stay in the sky —  
so she, and everyone who sees that trail, remembers:

**Tomorrow is still worth reaching.**

**Aster** = watcher · **Mira** = the one hope touches  
Not romance · not parent and child · **light and the person light finds**
