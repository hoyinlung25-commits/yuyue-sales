## 一、完整 Prompt（直接貼 Flova）

```text
GLOBAL LOCK — do not change across all frames:
Same fictional Hong Kong office worker man, age 28, slim build, short neat black hair,
white dress shirt with sleeves rolled to forearms, dark tie loosened, black trousers,
1990s black pager on belt, tired gentle face, natural skin texture, live-action only.

SCENE:
1996 Hong Kong night. Empty MTR subway platform after the last train has left.
Teal and magenta neon tube lights along the ceiling, wet glossy platform floor with
soft reflections, closed silver train doors on the right, yellow safety line, light rain
atmosphere on air. Mood: lonely but not scary — city pop melancholy, cinematic Wong
Kar-wai inspired color grade, subtle film grain, shallow depth of field.

ACTION (20 seconds total, one continuous shot):
0:00–0:04 — Wide static hold. Man stands alone near platform edge, hands in pockets,
looking at closed train doors. Only ambient flicker from neon.
0:04–0:08 — Very slow dolly-in (5% push). He exhales, glances down at pager on belt;
pager LED blinks once (subtle). He does NOT pick it up.
0:08–0:12 — Slow pan left with him as he turns away from train, begins walking toward
platform exit. Footsteps implied, coatless shirt only.
0:12–0:16 — Medium shot from behind: he stops, sees warm amber light leaking from a
narrow door in the tiled corridor ahead (hidden bar entrance). Rain mist in backlight.
0:16–0:20 — Hold medium-close from side profile: he looks at the door, slight pause,
then takes one slow step forward. Expression shifts from exhaustion to quiet curiosity.
End on this exact side-profile pose.

CAMERA:
Single continuous take feel. No cuts. No whip pans. No handheld shake.
Slow cinematic movement only. 24fps film motion blur.

LIGHTING:
Cool teal-magenta platform light transitioning to warm amber spill from door at end.
Deep shadows, soft halation on neon, wet surface specular highlights.

STYLE CONSTRAINTS:
Live-action vintage Hong Kong cinema still come alive. Photorealistic human.
NOT anime, NOT cartoon, NOT 3D render, NOT oversharpened AI skin.
NOT horror, NOT ghost, NOT supernatural effects.

LOOP OPTION (if needed):
First frame = last frame: same side-profile man, one foot forward toward amber door,
same head angle, same neon brightness. Subtle motion only: blink once every 8 seconds,
pager LED slow blink, rain particle drift.

NEGATIVE:
text overlays, subtitles, logos, watermark, extra people, crowd, running, fighting,
phone smartphone (use pager only), red vinyl record close-up, bartender visible,
time-lapse clouds, fast zoom, shaky cam, morphing face, changing clothes, changing age.
```
