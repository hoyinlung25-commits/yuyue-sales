# EP02《錯過末班車的那一夜》— Mureka 創作包

**主題：** 1990s City Pop / Urban Night Drive / Jazzhop  
**客人：** 1996 年加班到錯過末班車的上班族

## 曲目一《下一班人生》— 主歌 City Pop Jazzhop

**類型：** 男聲華語 · 約 4:00 · 86 BPM  
**功能：** 敘事主軸、YouTube 第一首、最容易被記住的 Hook

### Mureka Style Prompt

```text
Original 1990s Mandarin city-pop and jazzhop song, 86 BPM, 4/4,
around 4 minutes. Mature male mid-tenor with clear natural Mandarin,
slightly tired but warm delivery, restrained emotion and no theatrical
belting. Electric piano, melodic fingerstyle bass, clean chorus guitar,
soft drum machine, brushed snare, subtle analog synth pads and brief
muted saxophone phrases. Verses feel like late-night office fatigue;
chorus opens into hopeful city lights without becoming energetic pop.
Memorable melodic hook, sophisticated major-7th and minor-7th harmony,
warm cassette saturation and subtle 1990s Hong Kong night-drive mood.
End with electric piano, city ambience and cassette stop. No EDM,
trap, rap, bright modern synths, slap bass, flashy saxophone, modern pop
drums or dramatic climax.
```
