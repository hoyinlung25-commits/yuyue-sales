## 1 · Japanese Narration（旁白 · ElevenLabs）

**File:** `ep05_30sec_narration_ja.md`  
**Language:** 日本語 · first-person inner monologue · not anime narrator  

### Voice Design Prompt

```text
Create a Japanese male voice, early 20s, for a quiet cinematic fantasy inner monologue.

Character: Haruto Ren, a young hero who lost to a dragon and came to a hidden bar at
twelve-oh-seven to search for courage — not victory, not hype.

Tone: Clear Japanese tenor, intimate close-microphone, tired but not whiny. Literary and
restrained — JRPG hero after defeat, Kingdom Hearts / Final Fantasy sad monologue mood,
NOT shonen battle announcer, NOT Isekai comedy protagonist.

Pace: Slow. Half-beat pause after each sentence. Heavier on: 勇気をどこに落とした /
十二時七分 / 勇気を探しに来た.

Emotion: Exhaustion from battle → quiet search for courage. Not angry, not crying,
not inspirational speech. Like admitting truth to himself in rain.

Texture: Warm mid range, breath controlled, clear consonants, standard Japanese pronunciation.
Avoid: Anime hype voice, villain growl, old man narrator, robotic flat TTS, screaming.

Delivery: INNER MONOLOGUE — talking to himself, not explaining to audience.
```

### Test Lines（建 voice 後試讀）

```
村はまだ「勇者様」と呼ぶ。
僕だけ、勇気をどこに落としたか知っている。

ここでは、勝たなくていい。
僕は、勇気を探しに来た。
```

### TTS One-Liner

```text
Japanese male, early 20s, tired hero inner monologue, searching for courage after dragon defeat, slow literary pace, clear tenor, close mic, cinematic fantasy drama, not anime hype, not angry.
```
