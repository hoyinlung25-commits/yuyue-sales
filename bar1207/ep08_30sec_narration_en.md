# EP08 — 30 Second English Narration

**Character:** Aster · young star  
**Tone:** Sincere · young · not fairy-tale narrator  

---

## Narration Script

```
I was a young star.
I was taught to watch — never to fall.

One night I saw a girl in the rain.
She wasn't loud about giving up.
She was quiet — and that was worse.

The sky warned me:
once a falling star falls, it never goes home.

I found her near a door where the clock said twelve-oh-seven.
Not to save her with magic —
only to remind her: one more step is still allowed.

I burned the small self I was.
So she — and anyone who saw that light —
might remember:
tomorrow is still worth reaching.
```

---

## 中文大意

```
我是一颗年轻的星。被教只能守望，不能坠落。
我看见一个女孩在雨里，安静地把明天收起来。
天空警告我：流星一旦坠落，永远不能回家。
在十二时七分，我不是用魔法救她——
只是提醒她：还可以多走一步。
我燃尽小我，让所有人记得：明天仍值得抵达。
```
