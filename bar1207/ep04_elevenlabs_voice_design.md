## Narration Voice (Clara · Monologue)

### Voice Design Prompt

```text
Create a female English voice, mid-20s, for a quiet literary meditation monologue.

Tone: Soft, intimate, close-microphone. Neutral international English accent — not strongly
American or British, slightly European-international. Think wellness copywriter who is tired
of performing calm and has finally found real stillness.

Pace: Slow and measured. Natural pauses between sentences — half a beat, not dramatic.
Emotion: Quiet exhaustion turning into gentle clarity. Not sad, not motivational, not
yoga-instructor cheerful. Literary, restrained, expensive silence.

Texture: Warm low-mid range, breath present but controlled, no ASMR whisper, no vocal fry excess.
Delivery like reading a private journal aloud in an amber-lit bar at midnight.

Avoid: Broadcast announcer, influencer upspeak, gospel warmth, cartoon softness, robotic TTS flatness.
```

### Test Line (paste after voice created)

```
I used to think peace was something you subscribed to.
But the mind doesn't invoice you for stillness.
It only asks: are you here, or are you rehearsing being here?
```

## Singing Voice Reference (for Mureka alignment)

Mureka tracks use **whisper-sing**, not spoken narration. Use this block when comparing takes:

| Quality | Target |
|---------|--------|
| Register | Soft chest · light head mix · never belt |
| Accent | Neutral international English |
| Style | Literary ambient · not pop diva · not ASMR |
| Reverb | Long plate · bar cathedral · close mic dry core |
| Reference mood | Sade whisper · early Enya intimacy · no lyrics-forward pop |

`Same soft female English vocal as track 1, neutral accent, meditation whisper-sing, literary not pop.`
