# 《沒有狼的村莊》8 鏡極簡版｜一鍵操作清單

目標：用最少生成次數做出 **約 50–55 秒** YouTube Shorts。  
每一步只做一件事；方框內文字可直接複製貼上。

---

## 0. 開始前（只設一次）

| 項目 | 設定 |
|------|------|
| 工具 | Kling（Image-to-Video） |
| 畫幅 | **9:16** |
| 每鏡時長 | **5–6 秒** |
| Motion / 動態 | **低～中**（約 0.35–0.45） |
| 創意度 | **中低**（臉才穩） |
| 音樂 | Suno / Udio 一條 55–65 秒 |
| 剪輯 | CapCut 免費版 |

**Negative（每次生成都貼）：**
```text
photorealistic, live action, modern clothing, smartphones, cars, text artifacts, extra fingers, deformed face, morphing face, horror gore, explicit violence, blood, jump scare, 3D CGI plastic skin, watermark, logo, burned-in subtitles, ugly hands
```

---

## 1. 先做 4 張定裝圖（不要跳過）

> 只需 4 張。後面 8 鏡都用「圖生影片」。

### 定裝 A｜老人（給 M1、M8）
```text
Character reference sheet, elderly storyteller man, front and three-quarter view, deep wrinkles, kind knowing eyes, long silver beard, dark wool cloak with fur collar, holding a thick yellowed fairytale book, fireplace amber light on face, cool blue window light behind, hand-painted dark fairytale storybook animation, soft watercolor and ink edges, Nordic winter folklore mood, no photorealism, consistent face
```

### 定裝 B｜Elia（給 M3、M4、M5、M6）
```text
Character reference sheet, 12-year-old girl Elia, front and three-quarter view, freckles, large observant eyes, handmade winter coat, red wool scarf, brown hair in loose braid, holding sketchbook and charcoal pencil, innocent brave expression, hand-painted dark fairytale storybook animation, soft watercolor and ink edges, consistent facial features, no photorealism
```

### 定裝 C｜村莊（給 M2）
```text
Wide establishing matte painting of White Hollow village, tiny snowy wooden houses surrounded by dense dark forest, chimney smoke, fearful quiet winter atmosphere, Nordic folklore village, hand-painted dark fairytale storybook animation, cool snow blue palette, no photorealism, no modern objects
```

### 定裝 D｜畫冊結局（給 M7）
```text
Open sketchbook lying on snow, hand-drawn portraits of villagers, each casting a wolf-shaped shadow on the page, poetic tragic symbolism, no gore, moonlight and cold daylight mix, hand-painted dark fairytale storybook animation, high detail illustration
```

**檢查點：** Elia 臉固定、老人鬍鬚固定、紅圍巾固定。不滿意就重抽定裝，不要硬拿去生成影片。

---

## 2. 一鍵生成 8 鏡（照順序）

每鏡流程：
1. 上傳對應定裝／靜幀  
2. 貼 **Prompt**  
3. 貼 **Negative**  
4. 生成 5–6 秒  
5. 選最好的一條下載

---

### M1｜開頭鉤子（0:00–0:07）｜用定裝 A
**旁白：**「每個童話都說要提防狼。但如果……根本沒有狼呢？」

```text
Using this elderly storyteller character, he looks toward camera with a gentle knowing smile, thick fairytale book in hands, fireplace warm amber light on face, cold blue snowy window behind, subtle head turn then soft eye contact, slow cinematic push-in, quiet mysterious mood, hand-painted dark fairytale storybook animation, soft watercolor and ink edges, Nordic winter folklore, no photorealism
```

---

### M2｜村莊建立（0:07–0:13）｜用定裝 C
**旁白：**「White Hollow 被森林包圍。孩子都聽著狼的故事長大。沒人見過狼，卻每年有人消失。」

```text
Using this village image as base, wide establishing shot of snowy White Hollow surrounded by dense dark forest, chimney smoke rising slowly, overcast winter sky, fearful quiet atmosphere, slow lateral camera drift, painterly storybook matte painting animation, cool snow blue palette, no people close-up, no photorealism
```

---

### M3｜Elia 登場（0:13–0:19）｜用定裝 B
**旁白：**「今年輪到女孩 Elia。她最想畫出森林真正的樣子。」

```text
Using this girl Elia character, she sketches at a frosted window with charcoal, red wool scarf visible, hopeful curious gaze toward the dark forest outside, soft daylight, tender emotional close-up, slow tilt from sketchbook to her face, hand-painted dark fairytale storybook animation, consistent freckled face, no photorealism
```

---

### M4｜走進森林（0:19–0:26）｜用定裝 B（可先出一張「Elia＋森林」靜幀再動）
**旁白：**「她偷偷走進森林。沒有狼，只有鹿、狐狸，還有發光的花。」

```text
Elia walking deeper into a magical winter forest, same freckled girl with red wool scarf, gentle deer and fox watching calmly, soft bioluminescent flowers lighting the snow, peaceful sacred wonder, side-tracking follow camera, hand-painted dark fairytale storybook animation, no wolves, no photorealism
```

**可選靜幀先做（更穩）：**
```text
Elia with freckles and red wool scarf standing in magical winter forest among glowing flowers, calm deer nearby, moonlight on snow, storybook animation, same character design
```

---

### M5｜石碑真相（0:26–0:33）｜用定裝 B
**旁白：**「石碑寫著：狼早已離開。留下來的，只有人。」  
**字卡（剪輯加，勿讓 AI 寫字）：** `The wolves left long ago.` / `Only humans remained.`

```text
Elia kneeling before an ancient mossy stone stele in a forest clearing, wiping moss with her hand, moonlight revealing blank carved area, awe and shock on her face, slow push-in from medium to close, mystical revelation, hand-painted dark fairytale storybook animation, leave inscription unreadable for captions, no photorealism
```

---

### M6｜代罪羔羊（0:33–0:40）｜用定裝 B
**旁白：**「她把真相告訴村莊。他們卻說：她就是狼。」

```text
Symbolic storybook scene: Elia standing alone in a cold village square, crowd as dark silhouettes pointing at her, fear and accusation atmosphere, snow falling, non-graphic, no close-up violence, desaturated cold colors, slow push-in on Elia's tearful face, hand-painted dark fairytale animation, tragic scapegoat mood
```

---

### M7｜畫冊狼影（0:40–0:47）｜用定裝 D
**旁白：**「後來只找到她的畫冊。每一個村民的影子，都是狼。」  
**字卡：** `You needed the wolf—so you would never see yourselves.`

```text
Using this sketchbook image, open pages on snow, hand-drawn villagers casting wolf-shaped shadows, poetic horror symbolism, no gore, gentle wind moving page edges, slow tilt down then soft push-in to final page, hand-painted dark fairytale storybook animation, melancholic revelation
```

---

### M8｜金句收束（0:47–0:55）｜用定裝 A
**旁白：**「狼從來沒有住在森林裡……牠一直住在人心裡。」  
**畫面：**合書 → 燭滅 → 黑屏 1.5 秒

```text
Using this elderly storyteller character, he gently closes the thick fairytale book, candle flame flickering then extinguishing, final direct gaze to camera with quiet sorrow and wisdom, warm light dying into darkness, slow fade to black, hand-painted dark fairytale storybook animation, powerful moral ending, no photorealism
```

## 3. 音樂（做 1 次就好）

**Suno / Udio Prompt：**
```text
Cinematic dark fairytale narrative instrumental, 70 BPM, intimate piano and soft strings, faint choir pads, winter folklore atmosphere, melancholic but hopeful, no aggressive drums, quiet ending, 60 seconds
```

有人聲也可，但 **旁白優先**；歌聲小聲墊底即可。

---

## 4. CapCut 组装（15 分鐘內可完成）

依序拖入：`M1 → M2 → M3 → M4 → M5 → M6 → M7 → M8`

| 步驟 | 做什麼 |
|------|--------|
| 1 | 每鏡交界用 **0.3–0.5s 淡入淡出** |
| 2 | 鋪上音樂，結尾留 1 秒安靜 |
| 3 | 加旁白音軌（老人語氣、偏慢） |
| 4 | 字幕：白字＋薄黑描邊；金句字稍大 |
| 5 | M5 疊字卡兩行碑文 |
| 6 | M7 疊英文金句字卡 |
| 7 | 片頭 0.8s 小字：`森林寓言 Ep.01` |
| 8 | 片尾黑屏字幕：`下一集？留言你怕的是什麼` |
| 9 | 匯出 **1080×1920，30fps** |
